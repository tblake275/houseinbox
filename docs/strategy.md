# Strategy summary

Full doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/LVCSVLcdMcvTT15qUbPoTk

## Decision

Build the permit pre-review tool first. Start the plan catalog in month 4.

## Reasons

- Plans + materials list + cost-to-build report are a commodity. National plan sites sell a cost-to-build report for about $33.
- Capital-heavy housing startups failed: Katerra (over $2B raised, shut 2021), Veev ($600M raised, shut 2023), Mighty Buildings (put up for sale early 2025).
- The city's MCR is a measurable answer key. Builders hold the MCRs; no public dataset of MCR text exists.
- City of Austin already runs a free zoning AI pre-check (Archistar, launched 2025-09-30, new 1–3 unit builds). The open gap in Austin is the non-zoning disciplines: Technical, Tree, Fire, Drainage, and review triggers.

## First customers

Small builders, designers, and nonprofit affiliates (B2B). Not homeowners. (Assumption to confirm.)

## Competitors to watch

- City-side AI pre-check: Archistar, CivCheck (Clariti), CodeComply.Ai (CivicPlus)
- Applicant-side: PermittableAI, Greenlite, PermitFlow
- Plan marketplaces: Architectural Designs, Monster House Plans
- Supplier platform: Builders FirstSource myBLDR (partner, not target)
- Data: 1build (cost API), BuildZoom (permit and contractor data)
