# Glossary

- **AB+C** — Austin Build + Connect, the City of Austin permit portal.
- **AHFH** — Austin Habitat for Humanity.
- **AHJ** — Authority Having Jurisdiction: the city or county that issues the permit.
- **DSD** — City of Austin Development Services Department.
- **Finding** — One predicted comment from the House In Box tool.
- **IRC** — International Residential Code. Austin uses the 2024 edition with local amendments (LDC Chapter 25-12).
- **LDC** — Austin Land Development Code. Chapter 25-2 is zoning.
- **MCR** — Master Comment Report: the city's list of review comments for one review cycle.
- **PIA** — Texas Public Information Act request.
- **Precision** — Findings that match a city comment, divided by all findings.
- **Recall** — City comments that the tool also found, divided by all city comments.
- **Takeoff** — Material quantities measured from a plan set.
- **Tier 1 / 2 / 3 check** — Deterministic rule / completeness checklist / judgment item.
- **U0, U1, ...** — Review cycle numbers. U0 is the first submittal.
